2. Payment

All services require full payment upfront before any work begins. By submitting payment, the Client agrees to the full scope and price of the project as discussed. Accepted payment methods include PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, and Apple Pay.

No deliverables will be released until payment is confirmed. Djané Voice Solutions reserves the right to withhold completed work in the event of payment disputes.

3. Revisions

Revision policies vary by package:

  • Starter Package: 2 revision rounds included

  • Managed Monthly: Revisions included as part of ongoing service

Revisions must be consistent with the original agreed-upon scope. Requests that constitute a new project may be subject to additional fees. All revision requests must be submitted within 7 business days of delivery.

4. Turnaround Time

Standard projects are delivered within 2-3 business days from the date payment is confirmed and all required materials are received. Larger or more complex projects will be quoted individually with a mutually agreed upon timeline before work begins. Rush delivery may be available for an additional fee.

5. Client Responsibilities

To ensure timely delivery, Clients agree to:

  • Submit scripts as PDF files

Upon receipt of full payment, the Client is granted full rights to use delivered recordings for their stated business purpose. 7. Cancellations & Refunds

If a Client cancels before work has begun, a full refund will be issued. If work has already commenced, a partial refund may be issued based on work completed. Refunds are not issued once a final approved deliverable has been delivered.
